# Privacy Policy

How YT Screen Overlay accesses and processes Google and YouTube information, supports livestream overlays, and handles application account data.

**Effective:** October 11, 2026  ·  **Updated:** October 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

YT Screen Overlay is a desktop application that helps YouTube creators enhance livestreams through customizable overlays, viewer notifications, subscriber information, countdown animations, and engagement reminders.

This Privacy Policy describes information the application may access, process, store, and display when you use its features. The application is operated by [Developer or business name] (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

## 2. Information we access

### Google and YouTube information

Depending on the permissions you grant and the features you enable, YT Screen Overlay may access your YouTube channel identifier, channel name and profile information, subscriber statistics, livestream identifiers and status, live-chat messages and participant details, and supported Super Chat, membership, and subscriber-related events.

The precise information accessible depends on the application's approved OAuth scopes and YouTube API availability. YT Screen Overlay does not ask you to enter your Google password into the app.

### Application account information

Account records may include Google or YouTube identifiers, associated channel identifiers, account creation and login timestamps, authentication status, trial or subscription entitlements, trial-session usage, and administrative permission flags.

### Settings and local files

The app may process overlay configuration, notification settings, countdown durations, sound preferences, user-selected sound files, and other local preferences.

### Technical information

Operational diagnostics may include application version, connectivity state, error categories, and authentication failure details. Diagnostic logs should not intentionally contain passwords, authorization codes, access tokens, or PKCE verifiers.

## 3. How we use information

Information is used to authenticate users; identify connected YouTube channels; discover livestreams; display subscriber statistics, chat, Super Chat, and membership events; run countdowns and engagement reminders; save preferences; manage trial and feature access; maintain security; and troubleshoot application failures.

Google and YouTube data is intended to support the features users authorize and enable.

## 4. YouTube API Services

YT Screen Overlay uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube-connected features is also subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's privacy practices are described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

YT Screen Overlay is independently developed and is not an official Google or YouTube product.

## 5. Google authorization and permissions

YT Screen Overlay uses Google's authorization flow to request access to information needed for supported features. You can review permissions when connecting your account.

You can review or revoke access in your [Google Account third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revoking access may stop YouTube-connected features. Revoking Google authorization does not automatically delete application account records; deletion can be requested separately.

The application may also retain local settings, authentication state, temporary information, and diagnostics on your computer. YouTube information may be processed in memory or cached to support overlays and reduce unnecessary requests.

When you enable overlays in broadcasting software, selected content may be visible to livestream viewers, including chat messages, channel names, subscriber statistics, Super Chat or membership events, and engagement reminders. You control which supported overlays are enabled.
